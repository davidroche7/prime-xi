## Description (both stores; Apple ≤4000, Play ≤4000)

Every supporter has built the greatest XI in their head. The Perfect XI makes it a game with a
score.

A canonical greatest team — eleven players with their defining seasons, a formation, a manager and
his peak year — is hidden for each era of Liverpool FC's history. You build yours completely blind
and get counts-only feedback: players right out of eleven, seasons right out of eleven, ticks for
formation and manager. Never which picks. Working that out is the game. 98 is perfection.

Then take your team head-to-head. Pit your XI against the hidden greatest sides of Manchester
United, Real Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich, AC Milan, Ajax, Arsenal, Manchester City and
Everton. You get a scoreline and nothing else — and because every rating turns on the exact season
you picked, a legend in an off-year weakens your team. Is your greatest XI really the greatest in
Europe?

Need a warm-up? Guess the Red is the daily puzzle: one mystery player from 130 years of history,
six clues from hardest to easiest, a shareable score grid and a streak.

- Three eras: all-time, post-war, Premier League — each with its own hidden XI
- Head-to-head against nine of Europe's biggest clubs
- Counts-only feedback: knowledge, not luck
- Daily puzzle with streaks and spoiler-free sharing
- No account, no sign-up, works offline once loaded

Independent and unofficial: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any football club,
league or governing body. Player statistics are historical facts, from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA) and
lfchistory.net.
